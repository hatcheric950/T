# Intake checklist

Collect these before building anything. Don't invent answers — ask. If the user
is impatient, gather the **must-haves** and proceed with stated assumptions.

## Must-have

- **Product**: what is it, in one sentence? (e.g. "a $29/mo CRM for used-car
  dealers")
- **Price & billing**: amount, one-time vs subscription, billing period.
- **Buyer**: who pays? Consumer or business? Job title / segment if B2B.
- **Destination**: the URL the campaign/outreach sends people to. If none,
  flag that a landing page is step zero — ads to nowhere don't convert.
- **Budget**: ceiling for ad spend and/or tool credits. Confirm, never assume.
- **Geography**: countries/regions to target (affects targeting + compliance).

## Helpful

- **Goal & deadline**: framed as effort/throughput, not a guaranteed dollar sum.
- **Margin**: max sustainable cost-per-acquisition (CPA). A sale isn't a win if
  it cost more than it earns.
- **Existing assets**: brand, logo, photos, customer list, social accounts,
  ad account ID, CRM.
- **Proof**: testimonials, reviews, case studies, guarantees — the strongest
  small-business conversion levers.
- **Success metric**: the single number that says it worked.

## Small-business reality flags

- No landing page → fix that first (or use a link-in-bio / simple form).
- No ad account connected → can't launch paid; pivot to organic/outreach.
- Tiny budget (< ~$20/day) → favor organic + warm outreach over broad paid ads.
- Unrealistic deadline → reset expectations honestly before spending a cent.
